# Billing FAQ (fictional company "Acme Cloud")

## Refunds
Refunds are available within 30 days of a charge. Request one from Settings > Billing > Request refund. Refunds return to the original payment method in 5 to 10 business days.

## Duplicate charges
If you were charged twice for the same invoice, the duplicate is reversed automatically within 3 business days. If it is not, contact billing with the invoice number.

## Invoices
Invoices are emailed on the 1st of each month and can be downloaded as PDF from Settings > Billing > Invoices.

## Changing plans
Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated. Downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle.
