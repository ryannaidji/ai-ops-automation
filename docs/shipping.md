# Shipping FAQ (fictional company "Acme Cloud" hardware store)

## Delivery times
Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days in the continental U.S. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days.

## Tracking
A tracking number is emailed when the order ships. Tracking can take 24 hours to show the first scan.

## Lost or damaged packages
If tracking shows delivered but you have no package, wait 48 hours, then contact support for a replacement. Damaged items are replaced free of charge; send a photo within 7 days.

## Returns
Unused hardware can be returned within 30 days for a full refund. Return shipping labels are free.
