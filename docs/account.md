# Account FAQ (fictional company "Acme Cloud")

## Password reset
Use "Forgot password" on the login page. The reset link is valid for 1 hour. If the email does not arrive, check spam, then ask support to resend it.

## Two-factor authentication
2FA can be enabled in Settings > Security. If you lost your 2FA device, use one of your 10 backup codes. Without backup codes, support must verify your identity before disabling 2FA (takes up to 2 business days).

## Deleting an account
Account deletion is permanent after a 14-day grace period. Start it in Settings > Account > Delete account. Data exports are available until the grace period ends.

## Changing email address
Change your login email in Settings > Account. A confirmation link is sent to the new address.
